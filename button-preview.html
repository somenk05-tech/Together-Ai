Where it is today current

A · Polish the current style lowest risk · on-brand

Keeps the luxury pill. Adds gradient + soft shadow depth, a springy press, a proper loading spinner, and a ring focus state. Feels premium and tactile without changing the identity.

Variants
With icon & states

B · Modern SaaS refresh contemporary

Softer rounded-rectangles, sentence-case labels, tighter type, subtle 1px depth and quick transitions — a Linear/Stripe-style app feel. Biggest visual departure from today.

C · Bold & playful high energy

Larger targets, gradient fills, glow shadows and bouncy motion. Reads more consumer-social — good for a feed/reels product, louder than the current luxury tone.

D · Accessibility-first layerable

44px min hit targets, AA-contrast fills, thick visible focus outlines, respects reduce-motion. Can be combined with any look above — think of it as a rule set, not a separate style.
